From your notes to your exam

EXAMIVO doesn't generate random quizzes. It studies your material, adapts to your level, respects your examination format — then builds practice that actually prepares you.

STEP 01

Show it what you're studying

Upload a PDF or document, snap your handwritten notes, paste text — or just name a topic. EXAMIVO reads headings, definitions, formulas, processes and key terms.

STEP 02

Tell it your exam

Your class sets the language and depth. WAEC, NECO, JAMB, Common Entrance or a school test sets the question style, structure and marking expectations.

STEP 03

See your exam focus

Before a single question appears, EXAMIVO shows what it prioritized and why — based on your material and the examination format you selected.

STEP 04

Practice, grade, improve

Sit the exam under real conditions. EXAMIVO grades you, explains every answer, finds your weak areas and builds targeted follow-up practice.

An AI system built for examinations

Not a generic quiz generator. Not a chatbot. A purpose-built preparation engine.

Class-adaptive language

JSS1 gets clear, simple English. SS3 gets senior-secondary academic depth. University gets proper terminology. Never oversimplified, never needlessly dense.

Exam-format aware

Question types, structure and difficulty follow the examination you're actually sitting — from object sections to theory questions with proper marking depth.

Weak-area engine

Every attempt feeds a live map of your strong and shaky concepts. One tap turns them into a fresh, targeted practice set.

Real exam conditions

Timed or untimed. Flag questions. Navigate freely. No answers until submission — or instant feedback in Practice Mode, your choice.

Validated questions

Generated questions pass structural and content checks before you ever see them. Original, accurate, on-level — no recycled question banks.

Progress that follows you

Start as a guest — no signup wall. Create an account when you want your history, scores and study sessions synced across devices.
