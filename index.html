The EXAMIVO Architecture

Built specifically for examination mastery, not generic quizzes.

Every study document, handwritten note photo, or syllabus topic passes through a structured 9-stage pedagogical pipeline before a single question reaches your screen.

STAGE 01

Material & Content Analysis

Extracts headings, definitions, formulas, processes, examples, and diagrams from PDFs, documents, photos of notes, or pasted text.

STAGE 02

Class Profile Calibration

Adapts vocabulary, sentence complexity, and explanation depth to match Primary 1–6, JSS1–3, SS1–3, or University standards.

STAGE 03

Examination Format Alignment

Maps concepts to the exact question styles, timing, and cognitive demands of WAEC, NECO, JAMB / UTME, Common Entrance, or University exams.

STAGE 04

Exam Focus Prioritization

Highlights High Priority concepts and secondary revision topics before your test begins so you know what matters most.

STAGE 05

Question Generation & Validation

Crafts original multiple-choice, calculation, scenario, matching, and theory questions—and validates every option and explanation.

STAGE 06

Weak Area Engine & Retesting

Grades your submission, pinpoints exact concept weaknesses, generates "Study My Mistakes" revision sheets, and retests you until mastered.
